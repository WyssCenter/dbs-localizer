# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Wyss DBS Localizer to be a good experience for everyone: users who ask questions, people who report problems, contributors and maintainers. We welcome everyone regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the software, to programming or to DBS imaging.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and for the research the software supports.
- Protect patients' privacy: never post patient data or identifying information in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or e-mail address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issue tracker, pull requests and discussions, and any other channel used for the project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for example by posting from an official account or acting as a representative at an event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, or have any other concern, report it to the maintainer, Richard Köhler, at <richard.koehler@wysscenter.ch>. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the person who reports will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and will explain their reasons when appropriate.

Depending on how serious a breach is, the maintainers may respond with:

1. **Correction:** a private, written explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban from any public interaction within the community, for a pattern of violations or a single serious one, such as harassment.
