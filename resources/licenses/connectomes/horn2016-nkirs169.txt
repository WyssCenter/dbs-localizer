title: INDI Data Access Policy (verbatim)

INDI Data Access Policy

Given the increased amount of information provided in the INDI datasets, we are requiring that individuals register with the 1000 Functional Connectomes Project website on NITRC to gain access to the INDI datasets.
Upon successful registration, users will have full access to the datasets, and the right to unrestricted usage of the datasets for non-commercial purposes, just as before.

title: Enhanced NKI-RS data access, Neuroimaging Release (verbatim)

Neuroimaging Release

- Contains imaging data, physiological data acquired during scan acquisition (cardiac and respiratory), and limited phenotyping (age, sex, and handedness).

- No psychiatric, cognitive, or behavioral information is included.

- Immediately available to users (does not require a data usage agreement)

- Data is downloadable through Amazon Web Services or NITRC.
